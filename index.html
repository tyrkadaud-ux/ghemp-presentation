Our Vision

100% Halal Capital

We reject the traditional banking system built on Riba (usury). Our smart contracts facilitate ethical, profit-sharing investments that benefit both the farmer and the investor, in full compliance with Islamic finance principles.

Empowering Farmers

We provide farmers in the Republic of Tatarstan and beyond with direct access to global liquidity. By tokenizing their harvests, they can secure funding without the burden of high-interest loans.

Solana Blockchain

Built on Solana for its ultra-fast transaction speeds and microscopic fees. This ensures that every dollar goes into the real economy, not into network congestion.

Real World Assets (RWA)

We are not just creating a token; we are digitizing real value. Industrial hemp, grain, and agricultural products are brought on-chain, bridging the gap between Web3 and the real economy.

The GHEMP Story

Phase 1: The Genesis

Born from the idea to revive the industrial hemp sector that once powered Russia's economy for centuries. We united farmers and processing plants under a single vision.

Phase 2: Overcoming Barriers

We faced the bureaucratic wall of traditional funding. Rejected by local banks and state funds, we realized the system was broken and built for the few. We decided to build our own path.

Phase 3: Web3 & Islamic Finance

We pivoted to the blockchain. We chose Solana and embraced the principles of Islamic Finance. GHEMP is now a bridge between Russian agricultural potential and Islamic investors worldwide.
